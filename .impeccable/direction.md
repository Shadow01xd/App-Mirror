# TYU Android redesign

Scope: every existing native phone surface, Operate mode. Preserve the flow, behavior, names and black/yellow palette. Build directly in Compose as explicitly selected by the user.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal connection console. The link between devices is the central object; a composed device stage replaces the giant generic icon.

OWN-WORLD: Original black, charcoal and #F6CC45; native sans typography, compact monospaced metadata, exact outlined device geometry, 16 dp surfaces and 12 dp controls. Yellow marks actions and selection.

STORY: Identify the equipment, choose Monitor / Espejo / Bypass, configure or stop a service, and return to the same device state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact TYU masthead, left-aligned device title and connection state, wide phone/desktop schematic, equipment details, mode action, activity row, persistent mode selector. Welcome has larger brand typography. Signature motion: the link traces once on entry; an action compresses subtly on press.

FORM: Grounded candidate 5: precision connection console. Seed 3fee50b5. Candidates: native device manager, mixing desk, workstation dock, camera settings panel, precision connection console, topology map, equipment maintenance record. Consumer app and jackfield challengers are competitive in familiarity and link clarity, respectively, but lose specificity or phone legibility. Lexicon, rain garden, cyclorama and seven-segment lose audience fit and product clarity. Kept disciplines: comfortable touch targets, strong hierarchy, directional clarity, tonal separation, stable state positions and explicit linkage. The native platform and user's pinned colors take precedence.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

No shipping raster assets. Screenshot files are evidence only. Direct implementation is authorized; no further design approval is needed.
